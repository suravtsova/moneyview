Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. MoneyView works without sign-up. Your data is stored only on your device.

Where do exchange rates come from?
From ExchangeRate-API, with the European Central Bank (via Frankfurter) as a fallback. The source and update time are shown in the app under More → Exchange Rates.

Will converting change my original amounts?
Never. Every transaction keeps the amount and currency you actually paid; totals are converted only for display.

How do I back up or move my data?
Open More → Import & Export to export everything as CSV or JSON, and restore a JSON backup on another device.

How do I delete my data?
More → Import & Export → Delete All Data, or simply delete the app.
