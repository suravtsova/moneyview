MoneyView Privacy Policy

Effective: November 11, 2026

MoneyView is a personal finance app for iPhone and iPad. It is built so that your financial information stays with you. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

Data we collect

None. MoneyView has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking software. The developer does not collect, receive, store or sell any personal or financial data.

Data stored on your device

Accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, debts, receipt photos and settings you enter are stored only on your device. You can export all of it to CSV or JSON, or delete it, at any time in Settings → Import & Export. Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

Exchange rates

To show live exchange rates, MoneyView downloads public currency rate tables from ExchangeRate-API, with the European Central Bank (via Frankfurter) as a fallback. These requests contain no information about you or your finances. Like any internet request, they reveal your device's IP address to the rate provider, which handles it under its own privacy policy.

Camera, photos and Face ID

The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to attach a receipt to a transaction; images stay on your device. Face ID or your passcode is used only for the optional app lock, and is handled entirely by iOS — MoneyView never sees your biometric data.

Notifications, widgets and Siri

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Widgets and Siri read a summary that stays on your device, and you can hide amounts in Settings → Privacy.

Children

MoneyView does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example when new features are added, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
